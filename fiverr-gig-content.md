**A:** I primarily use Next.js, React.js, Modern JavaScript, CSS/Bootstrap/Tailwind, and Node.js for high performance and scalability.

**Q3: Can you convert Figma / Adobe XD / PSD designs to React/Next.js?**  
**A:** Absolutely! Send me your Figma, XD, or PSD file, and I will convert it into pixel-perfect, clean, and responsive code.

**Q4: Will my website be SEO-friendly and fast loading?**  
**A:** Yes. Next.js provides server-side rendering (SSR) and static site generation (SSG) out of the box, ensuring lightning-fast loading speeds and top search engine rankings.

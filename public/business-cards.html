🖨️ Card Printing Guide & Instructions (Card Banwanay Ke Liyay Tareeqa)

1. Standard Dimensions

UK / European Standard Business Card Size: 85mm × 55mm (Bleed size: 88mm × 58mm). Perfect for VistaPrint, InstantPrint, or any local UK print shop.

2. Recommended Paper & Finish

Paper: 400gsm or 450gsm Matt Laminated Silk Card.
Finish: Soft-touch velvet with Metallic Gold Foil on logo for Design 01.

3. Direct PDF Export

Click "Print Cards / Save as PDF" button above, select destination as "Save as PDF", paper size A4, and margins Default/None.

4. Working QR Code

The QR code is automatically linked to your website (www.arzairporttravel.co.uk) so clients can instantly scan it with their phone camera to book a ride!
